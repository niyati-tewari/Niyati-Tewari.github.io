About me

Founder, investor-in-training, and someone who believes good businesses can carry good stories.

My story

I grew up in Bangalore and started my first venture, Inaraa, in 2022 — working with artisan-led brands like Mandala and TerraBarn to bring handcrafted jewellery to a wider market. Running it taught me product, pricing, go-to-market and storytelling the hard way, and got Inaraa featured in outlets like The Hindu, YourStory and BusinessLine.

Alongside Inaraa I founded Bachpan Ke Khwaab, a non-profit running cyber-safety workshops and eye-screening drives with Sankara Eye Hospital, and hosted the Future Founders' Series, conversations with founders like Amit Agarwal (NoBroker) and Gaurav Dublish (Wildcraft).

Today I'm at Babson College studying technology entrepreneurship and finance. Outside work, you'll find me reading about investing, making digital art, or on a basketball court.
